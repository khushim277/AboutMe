About Me 🙋‍♀️

I'm a second-year Software Engineering and Commerce student at the University of New South Wales (UNSW). My interest in software started when I was fifteen and programmed an Arduino for the first time, and since then I've continued developing my skills across backend, frontend and software engineering overall.

I particularly enjoy backend development and working with APIs, data and application logic. Through university courses, personal projects and the UNSW Software Development Society Training Program, I've worked with various technologies including Python, TypeScript, JavaScript, FastAPI, Node.js, Express and React.

Outside of software, I'm also involved in several UNSW societies and currently serve as an Events Director for the Engineering Projects and Innovation Society.
